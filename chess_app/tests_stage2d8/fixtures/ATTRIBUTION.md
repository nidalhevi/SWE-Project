# Cburnett artwork

Artist: Colin M. L. Burnett.

The supplied SVGs contain the Cburnett artwork serialized in python-chess's
`chess.svg.PIECES`, retrieved on 2026-11-24:
https://python-chess.readthedocs.io/en/latest/_modules/chess/svg.html

The matching Cburnett set used by Lichess is documented here:
https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece/cburnett

Lichess attributes that artwork to Colin M. L. Burnett under GPL version 2 or
later. See https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/blob/master/COPYING.md .
GPL-2.0.txt is included alongside this notice. Keep the notice and SVG sources
when redistributing the generated templates. PNG templates are rasterizations
of these SVGs at 128 pixels, made with Inkscape. No Lichess logos are included.

Brown board colors in generated test fixtures are #f0d9b5 and #b58863.
Runtime recognition estimates each square's background from the image.

These templates and generated fixtures provide a reproducible starting point.
Their test accuracy is not evidence of accuracy on unseen browser screenshots.
